Yuzutsu

Samurais. Ninjas. Snow monkeys. Hot springs. You are Taomaru, a capybara monk who treats every battle like a minor inconvenience. As you casually stroll through a dangerous mission, outsmart and defeat your enemies with the ancient art of Yuzutsu.

Yuzutsu is a 2.5D puzzle-action game where fighting doesn't require brute force. Master the ancient technique of swapping places with enemies, hazards, and the world around you to orchestrate victory in your own way. Every decision creates satisfying consequences.

Core Features

  • Handcrafted Levels: Each level is carefully designed to reward your ingenuity while testing your zen as the puzzles become more complex.
  • Unlimited Replay: Instantly retry any puzzle as much as you like to discover new ways to solve it.
  • Five Distinct Regions: Journey through the five distinct regions of the Onsen Palace, where each area introduces new mechanics, enemies, challenges, and story moments.
  • Memorable Cast of Characters: Meet a colorful cast of eccentric characters whose dramatic personalities contrast with Taomaru's unwavering deadpan energy.
  • Hand-Drawn & Vibrant Visuals: Immerse yourself in a mystical folktale world brought to life through a charming hand-drawn art style.
